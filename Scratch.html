Scratch Maze Game

This is my second maze project created using Scratch.

use the arrow keys to move in respected direction

Dave the Beetle is one hungry little wanderer. Stumbling upon an unattended picnic, he thinks he’s found free food… but quickly realizes he’s trapped inside a maze of dangerous walls and obstacles. With the humans returning soon, what seemed like a feast turns into a race to escape. Guide Dave through the maze, avoid hazards, and make it out before it’s too late.

My name is Giovanni Sodano, and I am an expected graduate of a BSc in Kinesiology at the University of Calgary. I hope to pursue medical school and work toward becoming a physician. I have been inspired by sports my whole life, earning my second degree black belt in 2018 at Chan Lee Taekwondo.

Although I no longer practice Taekwondo, I stay active through the gym, running, and spending time with my family hiking. Outside of being active, I enjoy watching sports such as football, soccer, basketball, and hockey.

I am particularly interested in sport performance, health, and understanding how data and technology can be used to gain deeper insights into the human body.
